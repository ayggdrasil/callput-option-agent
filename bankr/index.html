Answers before the first click

Frequently asked questions.

Visible answers match the page's machine-readable FAQ contract.

Does opening the Bankr review submit a trade?

No. It opens prefilled transaction details in Bankr chat. You must explicitly send and approve them there.

What funds do I need?

Use Base USDC for premium or collateral and Base ETH for the displayed execution fee.

Does Callput receive or store my private key?

No. Callput prepares unsigned transaction data. Signing and broadcast remain in your Bankr wallet.

Are these broker-listed stock or ETF options?

No. They are synthetic on-chain options and do not represent broker-listed contracts, shares, securities, or ownership.

Why might no spread candidate appear?

Candidate availability depends on the live Callput market feed, selected direction, expiry, and risk constraints.

How does a Bankr agent connect to Callput?

Add https://mcp.callput.app/api/mcp as an HTTP MCP server with no authentication, then install the public Callput Skill.
